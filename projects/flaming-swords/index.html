Flaming Swords

Makes the Fire Aspect Enchantment show flames

Flaming Swords is a Minecraft resource pack that visually enhances combat by making weapons display animated flames when enchanted with Fire Aspect. The pack includes unique flame colors for Copper and Netherite swords and supports other weapons like the Mace and Bow when used alongside tixul's Enchantment Outlines. While it requires a Custom Item Textures (CIT) mod for Minecraft versions 1.21.4 and below, it also offers customizability through ResPackOpts to toggle specific features.

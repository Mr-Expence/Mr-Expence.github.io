Mr_Expence's PvP Overlay is a combat-oriented Minecraft resource pack designed to improve player-versus-player gameplay while maintaining a vanilla-like aesthetic. The pack features numerous visual optimizations for clearer combat visibility, including short swords, low shields, low fire, clearer water, and high-visibility cobwebs. Additionally, it modifies various items and effects by adding bow charge indicators, custom weapon enchantments, and reduced particle sizes to keep the screen uncluttered during fights.

Cosmetics+

Adds custom cosmetic models to the game

Minecraft Cosmetics Simplistic Vanilla
Download Modrinth CurseForge GitHub

Cosmetics+ is a Minecraft resource pack that introduces a variety of custom cosmetic models to enhance the game's visual appeal without altering gameplay mechanics. The project is currently in early alpha and requires the "Model Applier" mod for the helmets and armor to function properly, while the "Item Model Browser" is recommended to view all included models. Players can also contribute their own custom models by joining the creator's Discord server.
